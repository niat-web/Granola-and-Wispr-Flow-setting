Granola and Wispr Flow: Download and Setup Guide

Step-by-step instructions for downloading and installing two AI tools. Granola takes meeting notes and needs calendar and audio access. Wispr Flow is a voice dictation tool, not a meeting recorder.

Compiled from the official Granola help center and the Wispr Flow help center.

Part 1: Granola (meeting notes)

Granola listens to your meetings and writes notes for you. It works best when it can see the calendar you use for meetings.

Requirements: macOS 14 or later. A Google or Microsoft account for sign-in.

Install on macOS

  1. Go to the Granola website and download the app.

  2. Open Granola.dmg from your Downloads folder.

  3. Drag the Granola icon into your Applications folder.

  4. Open Granola from Applications, Launchpad, or Spotlight. If macOS shows the warning "Granola is from the internet. Are you sure you want to open it?", click Open. This warning is expected.

Install on Windows

  1. Go to the Granola website and download the app.

  2. Open Granola.exe from your Downloads folder.

  3. The Granola installer runs. When it finishes, open Granola from the Start Menu.

Sign in and grant permissions

  1. On the sign-in screen, click Sign in.

  2. Your web browser opens a new tab. Sign in with Google or Microsoft. Choose a work account if you have one, since Granola works best with the calendar you use for meetings.

  3. At the calendar permissions prompt, select Select all, then continue. Granola can then see your calendar events, including attendees, time, date, and title.

  4. On macOS: when the microphone and system audio popups appear, click Allow on each.
    On Windows: audio permissions are granted automatically.

    To review these later on a Mac: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone, or Screen & System Audio Recording. On Windows: Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone.

First meeting and home screen

  1. After your first sign-in, a two-minute demo meeting appears, with a tutorial from co-founder Sam. It appears only once. To watch it again, open grano.la/demo on a desktop with the Granola app installed, or use granola.ai/demo-meeting.

  2. The home view shows your upcoming meetings and the demo note.

Note: the official setup page does not describe a separate "start recording" button. Check the Granola help center for how recording starts in your meetings.

Part 2: Wispr Flow (voice dictation)

Wispr Flow turns your speech into text in any app. You hold a shortcut, speak, and release. It is not a meeting recorder, so it will not take notes from a call.

Requirements: macOS 12.0 (Monterey) or later on a Mac. Check your version and chip under Apple menu > About This Mac. Apple Silicon and Intel Macs need different downloads.

Install on macOS

  1. Go to wisprflow.ai and download the build that matches your Mac's chip.

  2. Open the downloaded .dmg file.

  3. Drag the Wispr Flow icon onto the Applications folder, then close the installer window.

  4. Open Wispr Flow from Applications. On a fresh install, a menu bar icon appears on the right side of the menu bar.

    Keep the name: the app must stay named Wispr Flow.app and sit directly in /Applications, ~/Applications, or /System/Applications. A renamed app, or one inside a subfolder, will not launch.

Install on Windows

  1. Go to wisprflow.ai and download the Windows installer.

  2. Run the installer.

  3. Launch Wispr Flow from the Start menu. A system tray icon appears when it is running.

First-run setup (both platforms)

  1. Click Sign in via browser. You can sign in with Google, Apple, Microsoft, SSO, or email and password. If the session times out, click Sign in via browser again.

  2. Permissions. On Mac, click Allow on "Allow Flow to insert spoken words" (Accessibility) and on "Allow Flow to use your microphone" (Microphone), then click Continue. On Windows, you need microphone access. If you denied a prompt, enable Flow under Privacy & Security in System Settings (Mac) or Settings (Windows).

    Optional, Mac only: Input Monitoring under System Settings > Privacy & Security, only if you want Caps Lock alone as your shortcut.

  3. Answer the setup questions and review the Privacy notice. If you choose "Other," type a short description to continue.

  4. Test your microphone. Speak, and confirm the bars rise. To switch inputs, use Change microphone or Find the right mic for you. If you see "No microphone found," connect a mic in Sound Settings. If you see "In use by another app," check Sound Settings or restart Flow.

  5. Set your shortcut. Click Edit shortcut, then choose Push to talk or Hands-free. Macs with an fn key default to fn. Other machines default to Ctrl+Opt.

  6. Choose languages. Select the dictation languages you use, or leave automatic detection on. You can mix languages in one sentence.

  7. Practice. Press the shortcut keys together, speak, and release as instructed. You can skip any demo. On Windows, a checkmark finishes dictation and X cancels it.

  8. Finish. Choose a data preference, then click Explore Wispr Flow. Setup is complete when the Flow Hub opens.

    Data setting: "Improve the model for everyone" is on by default for Free and Pro accounts. Choosing "Don't use my data for training" does not turn off cloud storage of dictations or delete local history. Check each setting separately.

After setup, hold your shortcut in any app and speak. Double-tap it for Hands-free mode.

Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
Granola says "from the internet" and will not open (Mac)Click Open. This warning is expected.
Permission prompt was denied (Granola or Wispr Flow)Enable the app under Privacy & Security in System Settings (Mac) or Settings (Windows).
Wispr Flow does not launchConfirm the app is named Wispr Flow.app and is in an Applications folder.
Wispr Flow shows "No microphone found"Connect a microphone, then check Sound Settings.
Wispr Flow sign-in times outClick Sign in via browser again.
